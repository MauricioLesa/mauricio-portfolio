I'm a software engineer from Cochabamba, Bolivia. I studied systems engineering, and for my final degree project I spent a year and a half building a video frame interpolation model: a small neural network in PyTorch that takes two frames of a video and generates the frame in between.

At Jalasoft I worked as a software engineer for two US clients, remotely and in English. Most of the work was on large systems that already existed: adding REST APIs and features, speeding up slow screens, and changing code safely in applications other people depend on. I also took part in migrating a .NET Razor front end to React with Claude Code skills, which is the first case study on this site.

What I like most is taking a real problem from a client, understanding it, and shipping something that works and keeps working. Right now that means AI features built like any other production software: specified, tested with evals, monitored, and cheap to run. This site is where I practice that in the open.
